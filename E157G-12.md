SUBJECT: Change 1 to Logic and Equations for the RTCC Lunar Ascent Predictor
Reference: MSC Internal Note No. 68-FM-18, "Logic and Equations for the
RTCC Lunar Ascent Predictor, " dated January 22, 1968.
Since the publication of the referenced document, an error in synchronizing
state vectors is indicated due to large time tag differences. At the time
of publication, it was thought that the time tag difference was less than
two seconds, so a short free fall method was adequate. The time difference
between the state vector is now thought to be more like four to six seconds
and, therefore, the present free fall scheme couldn't handle it.
The free fall logic to synchronize state vectors has been removed and the
present plans are to use an interpolation scheme to do the synchronization.
